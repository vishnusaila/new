Fun Fact

AI was first coined in 1956! Artificial Intelligence, or AI, was first coined in 1956 at the Dartmouth Conference by John McCarthy. This event marked the official beginning of AI as a field of study. Since then, AI has evolved rapidly, influencing various sectors like healthcare, education, technology, and business with intelligent, machine-based solutions.
